Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "DoodlePop! Magic Physics Draw" (hereinafter referred to as the "App"), developed and published by Onion Works (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our").

We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of young children and their parents. This App strictly adheres to the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

Core Privacy Guarantee: The App does NOT collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (such as names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or contacts).

1. Device Permissions & Sensor Data

To provide an engaging, responsive physics playground, the App accesses the following device hardware features locally:

  • Accelerometer & Gyroscope: Used solely in real-time to adjust the direction and intensity of gravity within the drawing canvas. This sensor data is processed entirely in memory on your device and is never stored, tracked, or transmitted over any network.
  • Shake Gesture Detection: Detects physical shaking of the device to reset the canvas and clear drawings.
  • Photo & Storage Access (Save & Share): Only utilized when the user chooses to save their artwork or export animated GIFs to the device’s local gallery, or share via the native OS share sheet.

2. Advertising & Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

To support ongoing maintenance and provide the App for free, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

As a certified family and child-directed application, we enforce strict compliance safeguards in accordance with Google Play Families Policies and COPPA:

  • Child-Directed Treatment (tagForChildDirectedTreatment): All ad requests flag child-directed treatment. Behaviorally targeted ads, cross-app tracking, and interest-based profiling are completely disabled.
  • Content Rating G (General Audience): Advertisements are strictly limited to family-friendly, general audience content appropriate for young children.
  • Parental Gate Protection: Access to rewarded video ads (used to unlock optional tools and pens) is gated behind a math-based parental confirmation lock to prevent inadvertent taps by young children.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please visit:
Google Advertising Privacy & Terms

3. Local Storage

Unlocked tools, user preferences (such as color palette choices and canvas background theme), and saved sketches are stored securely and exclusively on your local device storage. No user drawings or configuration data are uploaded to our servers.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect legal amendments, platform policy updates, or feature enhancements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page and will take effect immediately upon publication.
